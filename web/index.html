🚀 Wi-Fi Hardware & Kernel Accelerators

256-QAM (TurboQAM) Modulation

Unlocks 256-QAM high-density constellation on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. Boosts 2.4 GHz peak speeds from 150/300 Mbps to 200/400 Mbps (+33% throughput).

Ultra-Low Latency Continuous Access Mode (CAM)

Disables 802.11 power-saving sleep cycles. Transceiver remains continuously active, eliminating 50–150ms periodic ping spikes for competitive gaming.

Auto Performance Monitor (autoPerfCfg)

Enables continuous hardware link rate adaptation monitoring for instantaneous PHY rate switching under interference.

Dual Band Dual Concurrent (DBDC) Concurrency

Hardware-level concurrent dual-band operation enabling simultaneous 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz operations.

5GHz & Hotspot Speed Booster (2x2 MIMO Spatial Streams)

Unlocks 2 Spatial Streams across Station and SoftAP. Stock Samsung limits Nss=1 (433 Mbps limit). Unlocking Nss=2 doubles peak 5GHz throughput to 866.7 Mbps.

5GHz Channel Bandwidth Mode (ApBw & Sta5gBw)

Hardware channel bandwidth mode for 5GHz radio. Mode 3 unlocks 160 MHz ultra-wide channel capability (up to 1733 Mbps PHY).

All-Channel & DFS Restrictions Override

Systemless overlay of indoorchannel.info and wpa_supplicant_overlay.conf eliminates DFS wait locks on channels 52–144 and UNII-3 149–165.
